# Intent Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 28, 2026

Intent is a local-first macOS productivity app. It helps you choose three concrete outcomes for the day and keeps them visible from the menu bar.

## Data Stored on Your Mac

Intent stores your plans, settings, completion state, local history, event logs, and validation cache on your Mac. This data is not synced to an Intent account.

## Data Sent for Validation

If remote validation is enabled, Intent sends the three objective strings you enter, an app client version, an install identifier, your local date key, and your timezone identifier to the Intent Validation API so the app can evaluate whether the objectives are concrete enough to complete. Remote validation can be disabled in Settings.

The validation API may process technical request information, such as IP address and request metadata, for rate limiting, abuse prevention, security, and service operation. Raw objective text is not stored by the server in normal operation.

## No Accounts, Payments, or Tracking

Intent does not require an account, process payments, use advertising, sell personal information, or use third-party tracking for advertising.
